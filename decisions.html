核心案例中，决策者（政府、央行、国际机构或个人）在关键时刻做出的决定。每一点都附有英文维基原文的逐字摘录，经脚本核对确为原文的精确子串；中文概述不超出摘录内容。「当时的其他选项或争论」只在原文明确写到时才列出。本页只记录发生了什么，不评价决定对错，也不推断「本应如何」。

郁金香狂热(1637)

  1. 约 1637 · 在阿姆斯特丹集会的各方代表：决定折中：1636年12月前的合约有效，之后的合约可支付价格10%的费用取消。
    They decided on a compromise where all contracts entered before December 1636 would be binding, but later contracts could be cancelled by paying a fee amounting to 10% of the price.

    来源：Tulip mania

  2. 约 1637 · 荷兰立法机构：决定取消全部合约，以便夏季重新交易。
    The Dutch legislature decided to cancel all contracts to allow fresh deals to be struck during the summer.

    来源：Tulip mania

密西西比泡沫(1720)

  1. 1719 · 摄政王：1719年5月21日摄政王颁布敕令认可合并交易，并将合并实体命名为印度公司。
    On 21 May 1719, a royal edict issued by the Regent endorsed the transaction and gave the name Compagnie des Indes to the merged entity.

    来源：John Law's Company

  2. 1720 · 法国政府：1720年5月，在密西西比公司和受让人投诉后，法国政府禁止向路易斯安那遣送囚犯。
    In May 1720, after complaints from the Mississippi Company and the concessioners about this class of French immigrants, the French government prohibited such deportations.

    来源：John Law's Company

  3. 约 1721 · 法国国家：重组中，法国国家向公司支付5.14亿里弗赔偿，并保留原有股东。
    As part of the restructuring, the French state paid the company an indemnity of 514 million livres to make it whole, and it kept its prior shareholders

    来源：John Law's Company

南海泡沫(1720)

  1. 1719 · 南海公司：宣布将1719年圣诞股息推迟12个月发放。
    Perhaps the first sign of difficulty came when the South Sea Company announced that its Christmas 1719 dividend would be deferred for 12 months.

    来源：South Sea Company

  2. 1720 · 英国下议院：经 John Hungerford 提议，设立由其主持的委员会调查泡沫公司。
    On 22 February 1720, John Hungerford raised the question of bubble companies in the House of Commons, and persuaded the House to set up a committee, which he chaired, to investigate.

    来源：South Sea Company

  3. 1720 · 南海公司：在崩盘前推动《1720年泡沫法》，该法禁止未获皇家特许状设立股份公司。
    The Bubble Act 1720 (6 Geo. 1 c. 18), which forbade the creation of joint-stock companies without royal charter, was promoted by the South Sea Company itself before its collapse.

    来源：South Sea Company

1857年危机(首次全球金融危机)

  1. 1857 · 英国政府：1857年11月12日英国政府被迫再次暂停《1844年银行特许法》。
    The government was forced to suspend the Bank Charter Act 1844 again on November 12.

    来源：Panic of 1857

  2. 1859 · 美国总统布坎南：布坎南认定纸币体系是恐慌根源，决定撤回20美元以下银行券的使用。
    President James Buchanan announced that the paper-money system seemed to be the root cause of the Panic and then decided to withdraw the usage of all bank notes under twenty dollars.

    来源：Panic of 1857

  1. 1873 · 英格兰银行：危机来临时英格兰银行将利率提高至9%。
    When the crisis came, the Bank of England raised interest rates to 9 percent.

    The bill was vetoed by President Grant.

    当时的其他选项或争论：国会主张通过印钞增加通胀、减轻债务价值。

1907年恐慌(美联储成立契机)

  1. 1907 · J.P. 摩根及其同事：审查尼克博克信托账目后认定其已资不抵债，未出手阻止挤兑。
    Morgan and his associates examined the books of the Knickerbocker Trust and decided it was insolvent, so they did not intervene to stop the run.

    来源：Panic of 1907

  2. 约 1907 · 美国财政部长 Cortelyou：周四上午向纽约多家银行存入约2500万美元。
    On Thursday morning Cortelyou deposited around $25 million into a number of New York banks.

    来源：Panic of 1907

  3. 约 1907 · J.P. 摩根：为避免纽约市破产发出灾难性信号，签约购买3000万美元市政债券。
    In an effort to avoid the disastrous signal that a New York City bankruptcy would send, Morgan contracted to purchase $30 million worth of city bonds.

    来源：Panic of 1907

  4. 1908 · 美国国会：1908年5月通过《奥尔德里奇-弗里兰法》，设立国家货币委员会调查恐慌并提出银行监管立法。
    In May 1908, Congress passed the Aldrich–Vreeland Act, which established the National Monetary Commission to investigate the panic and to propose legislation to regulate banking.

    来源：Panic of 1907

魏玛共和国恶性通胀(1921–1923)

  1. 1914 · 德国：一战爆发时暂停金本位以支付战争开支。
    To pay for the large costs of the First World War, Germany suspended the gold standard (the convertibility of its currency to gold) when the war broke out in 1914.

    来源：Hyperinflation in the Weimar Republic

  2. 1923 · 德国政府：法比占领鲁尔后，下令在鲁尔实行消极抵抗。
    The German government's response was to order a policy of passive resistance in the Ruhr, with workers being told to do nothing which helped the French and Belgians in any way.

    来源：Hyperinflation in the Weimar Republic

  3. 1923 · 农业部长 Hans Luther：提议以黄金替代黑麦作为债券指数，促成地租马克发行。
    Agriculture Minister Hans Luther proposed a plan that substituted gold for rye and led to the issuance of the Rentenmark

    当时的其他选项或争论：Helfferich 曾提议以黑麦价格挂钩债券支持的“黑麦马克”，因黑麦纸马克价格波动大被否决。

    The plan was rejected because of the greatly fluctuating price of rye in paper marks.

    来源：Hyperinflation in the Weimar Republic

  1. 1929 · 纽约主要银行家：推选交易所副总裁 Richard Whitney 代表银行家行事。
    They chose Richard Whitney, vice president of the Exchange, to act on their behalf.

  2. 1932 · 美国参议院：设立 Pecora 委员会研究股灾成因。
    In 1932, the Pecora Commission was established by the U.S. Senate to study the causes of the crash.

    来源：Wall Street crash of 1929

  3. 1933 · 美国国会：通过《1933年银行法》（格拉斯-斯蒂格尔法），分离商业银行与投资银行。
    Congress responded to the events by passing the Banking Act of 1933 (Glass–Steagall Act), which separated commercial and investment banking

    来源：Wall Street crash of 1929

    来源：Great Depression

匈牙利 pengő 恶性通胀(1945–1946)

  1. 1946 · 匈牙利政府：1946年1月1日引入“税收彭戈”（adópengő），最初作为预算规划用的指数化记账单位。
    The Hungarian government introduced the adópengő (lit. 'tax pengő') on 1 January 1946, originally as an indexed unit of account for budget planning

    来源：Hungarian pengő

第一次石油危机(1973)

  1. 1969 · 美国总统尼克松：舒尔茨委员会建议以关税取代石油进口配额，但尼克松因强烈政治反对决定保留配额。
    Shultz's committee recommended that the quotas be abolished and replaced with tariffs, but Nixon decided to keep the quotas due to vigorous political opposition.

    当时的其他选项或争论：舒尔茨委员会建议废除配额、改用关税。

    Shultz's committee recommended that the quotas be abolished and replaced with tariffs

    In October 1973, the Organization of Arab Petroleum Exporting Countries (OAPEC) announced that it was implementing a total oil embargo against countries that had supported Israel

    来源：1973 oil crisis

  3. 1973 · 日本政府：1973年12月日本政府下令主要产业立即削减20%用油和电力；并拒绝企业放宽污染管制的要求。
    In December 1973, it ordered an immediate 20% cut in oil use and electric power to Japan's major industries, and cutbacks in leisure automobile usage.

    当时的其他选项或争论：原文提到日本企业要求政府放宽空气和水污染管制，政府拒绝。

    Seeking to take advantage of the crisis, Japanese business called on the government to relax its controls on air pollution and water pollution. The government refused.

    来源：1973 oil crisis

拉丁美洲债务危机(1975–1982)

  1. 1982 · 墨西哥（财政部长席尔瓦-埃尔索格）：1982年8月墨西哥宣布无法偿债，单方面宣布90天延期偿付，并请求重新谈判还款期和新贷款。
    Mexico stated that it could not meet its payment due dates, and announced unilaterally a moratorium of 90 days; it also requested a renegotiation of payment periods and new loans in order to fulfill its prior obligations.

    来源：Latin American debt crisis

  2. 1982 · 多数商业银行：墨西哥主权违约后，多数商业银行大幅削减或停止对拉美的新增贷款。
    In the wake of Mexico's sovereign default, most commercial banks reduced significantly or halted new lending to Latin America.

    来源：Latin American debt crisis

  3. 年份不详 · 拉美多数国家：作为对危机的回应，多数国家放弃进口替代工业化模式，转向出口导向工业化战略。
    In response to the crisis, most nations abandoned their import substitution industrialization (ISI) models of economy and adopted an export-oriented industrialization strategy

    当时的其他选项或争论：原文提到智利和哥斯达黎加等例外国家采取了改良主义战略。

    although there were exceptions such as Chile and Costa Rica, which adopted reformist strategies.

    来源：Latin American debt crisis

  1. 1994 · 总统塞迪略／墨西哥央行：1994年12月20日塞迪略宣布央行将比索贬值13%至15%。

    Clinton decided nevertheless to seek Congressional approval for a bailout and began working with Summers to secure commitments from Congress.

阿根廷经济危机(2001–2002)

  1. 2001 · 内阁首席/经济部长卡瓦略（Cavallo）：2001年7月对全体公务员实施最高13%的全面减薪并同等削减政府养老金。

  2. 2001 · 国际货币基金组织（IMF）：2001年12月5日拒绝发放13亿美元贷款分期款，理由是阿根廷政府未达成预算赤字目标。
    The crisis intensified when, on 5 December 2001, the IMF refused to release a US$1.3 billion tranche of its loan, citing the failure of the Argentine government to reach its budget deficit targets

  3. 2002 · 杜阿尔德（Duhalde）政府：2002年1月经过反复斟酌后放弃了实行十年的固定汇率。

    来源：1998–2002 Argentine great depression

  4. 2005 · 基什内尔（Kirchner）：2005年12月15日继巴西类似举措之后，宣布阿根廷将全额偿还对IMF的债务。
    On 15 December 2005, following a similar action by Brazil, Kirchner suddenly announced that Argentina would pay the whole debt to the IMF.

    来源：1998–2002 Argentine great depression
