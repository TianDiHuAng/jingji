寻租

通过操纵公共政策或经济条件来增加既有财富、而不创造新财富的行为。

理论理论制度全球L2 · 重要

概述

（据 Wikipedia「Rent-seeking」摘要归纳）Rent-seeking is the act of growing one's existing wealth by manipulating public policy or economic conditions without creating new wealth. Rent-seeking activities have negative effects on the rest of society. They lead to reduced economic efficiency due to misallocation of resources, stifled competition, lower wealth creation, a loss of government revenue, increased income inequality, increased debt levels, the risk of increased corruption and cronyism, the decline in public trust in institutions, and potential socioeconomic and institutional decline. 具体定义边界与学术争议待核实。

与其他案例的相似与不同

与**寻租**相近但需与**利润**区分：利润来自创造新价值（生产、创新、效率改进），寻租来自**重新分配**既有价值（游说关税、牌照限制、补贴争取）。与**腐败**的区别在合法性：寻租多通过**合法**的政治影响实现（游说、旋转门），腐败涉及非法交易。在金融危机中，寻租的典型形态是"太大而不能倒"带来的隐性补贴——机构通过规模获得保护，而非通过效率。
