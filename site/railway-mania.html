铁路狂热(1840年代英国)

1840年代英国铁路投资狂潮（Railway Mania），大量资本涌入铁路股票形成泡沫，随后崩盘。

泡沫20世纪上半欧洲L2 · 重要

概述

Railway Mania was a stock market bubble in the railway industry of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Ireland in the 1840s. It followed a common pattern: as the price of railway shares increased, speculators invested more money, which further increased the price of railway shares, until the share price collapsed.
