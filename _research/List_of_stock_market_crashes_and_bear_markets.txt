This is a '''list of stock market crashes and bear markets'''. The difference between the two lies in the rate of decline and timeframe over which they occur. [[Stock market crash]]es are quick and brief, while [[bear market]]s are slow and prolonged. The two do not necessarily happen in conjunction.

|[[Black Monday (1987)|Black Monday]]
|{{dts|format=dmy|1987|Oct|19}}
|{{flag|United States}} <br/> {{flag|New Zealand}}
|Infamous stock market crash that represented the greatest one-day percentage decline in U.S. stock market history, culminating in a bear market after a more than 20% plunge in the [[S&P 500]] and [[Dow Jones Industrial Average]]. Among the primary causes of the chaos were program trading and illiquidity, both of which fueled the vicious decline for the day as stocks continued lower, even as volume grew lighter. Today, [[Trading curb|circuit breakers]] are in place, to prevent a repeat of Black Monday. After a 7% drop, trading would be suspended for 15 minutes, with the same 15 minute suspension kicking in after a 13% drop. However, in the event of a 20% drop, trading would be shut down for the remainder of the day.
In New Zealand, the stock market continued to crash for the next 5 months, down to 60%.
