This is a '''list of banking crises'''. A banking crisis is a [[financial crisis]] that affects banking activity. Banking crises include [[bank run]]s, which affect single banks; banking panics, which affect many banks; and systemic banking crises, in which a country experiences many defaults and financial institutions and corporations face great difficulties repaying contracts.<ref name=imf2008>{{cite web |url=http://www.imf.org/external/pubs/ft/wp/2008/wp08224.pdf |access-date=2008-09-29 |author= Laeven L, Valencia F |title= Systemic banking crises: a new database |version= IMF WP/08/224 |publisher= International Monetary Fund |date=2008}}</ref> A banking crisis is marked by bank runs that lead to the demise of financial institutions, or by the demise of a financial institution that starts a string of similar demises.<ref name=Reinhart>{{cite book |author=Reinhart C, Rogoff K |title=This Time is Different: Eight Centuries of Financial Folly |chapter=Varieties of crises and their dates |chapter-url=http://mpra.ub.uni-muenchen.de/17452/1/MPRA_paper_17452.pdf |year=2009 |access-date=2009-11-28 |pages=3–20 |publisher=Princeton University Press |isbn=978-0-691-14216-6 }}</ref>
 
== Bank runs ==
{{main|List of bank runs}}

A [[bank run]] occurs when many bank customers withdraw their [[Deposit account|deposits]] because they believe the bank might fail. There have been many runs on individual banks throughout history; for example, some of the [[2008–2009 bank failures in the United States]] were associated with bank runs.

== Banking panics and systemic banking crises ==

=== 17th century ===

* Dutch banking crisis of 1672, [[Rampjaar]] financial turmoil. Middelburg bank suspended payments amid wartime runs. The [[Bank of Amsterdam]] faced a bank run but remained solvent.<ref>{{Cite book |last=Van Nieuwkerk |first=Marius |title=The Bank of Amsterdam: on the origins of central banking |last2=Kroeze |first2=Cherelt |date=2009 |publisher=Sonsbeek |others=Nederlandsche bank |isbn=978-90-78217-13-8 |location=Arnhem |pages=135}}</ref>

*[[Panic of 1792]], New York
